# ADR-001: Bounded-context strategy

## Status

Proposed

## Context

Define boundaries from code, transactions, runtime, and ownership; do not mirror CRUD entities.

## Evidence

See current-state verification, bounded-context map, ownership maps, and repository paths cited there.
The completed Strategy seam plus new in-process Risk and Market Data facades validate package-first boundaries; Backtest remains the preferred first deployable candidate.

## Decision

Use cohesive contexts recorded in the context map.

## Alternatives considered

Historical six-service split; scaffold-driven split.

## Consequences

Better cohesion; slower extraction.

## Risks

Incorrect boundaries could split invariants.

## Rollback strategy

Keep modular monolith.

## Approval required

Human review.

# ADR-025: Backtest staging gates and CI promotion

Status: Proposed

The integration job remains non-blocking until deterministic persisted parity and independent two-process tests are repeatable, rollback is exercised, flake rate is accepted by the team, and monitoring operators approve staging thresholds. Duplicate claims, stale-epoch claims, parity mismatches, authorization regressions, cross-user exposure, cancellation-order violations, unexplained recovery, or unsafe shutdown are hard canary blockers.
